FAQ

When was Anupama Parameswaran born?
She was born on February 18, 1996.
Where is she from?
She is from Irinjalakuda, Thrissur district, Kerala, India.
What is her education?
She studied Communicative English at CMS College Kottayam, Kerala, but discontinued to pursue a career in acting.
What was her debut movie?
Her debut was the Malayalam blockbuster Premam (2015), where she played the role of Mary George.
